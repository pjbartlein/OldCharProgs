### 2007_NAChar

Analysis of North American charcoal (and pollen and tree-cover) data and initial development of fixed-size window lowess-style locally weighted regression. Preliminary development of regional and global syntheses as in: 

Marlon, J.R., Bartlein, P.J., Carcaillet, C., Gavin, D.G., Harrison, S.P., Higuera, P.E., Joos, F., Power, M.J., Prentice, I.C., 2008. Climate and human influences on global biomass burning over the past two millennia. Nature Geosci 1, 697–702. [https://doi.org/10.1038/ngeo313](https://doi.org/10.1038/ngeo313)
