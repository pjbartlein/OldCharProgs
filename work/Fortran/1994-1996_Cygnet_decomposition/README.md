### 1994-1996_Cygnet_decomposition

First iterations of background-and-peaks decomposition, including interpolation to constant time intervals and locally weighted estimation of the background component.

These programs became the basis for the decompostion in 
* Long, C.J., Whitlock, C., Bartlein, P.J., Millspaugh, S.H., 1998. A 9000-year fire history from the Oregon Coast Range, based on a high-resolution charcoal study. Canadian Journal of Forest Research 28:774-787.
* Millspaugh, S.H., Whitlock, C., Bartlein, P.J., 2000. Variations in fire frequency and climate over the past 17 000 yr in central Yellowstone National Park. Geology 28:211-214.
